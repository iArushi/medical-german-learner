PRD: Medical German Practice for Nurses
1. Problem

How might we help an Indian nurse at A1 to A2 German learn, remember and repeatedly practise medical German vocabulary in a simple, engaging way, so she never feels finished after one quiz?

2. User

Given in the brief: an Indian nurse, A1 or A2 German, preparing to work in Germany. Her phone habits, free time and interest in competing are hypotheses to test with real users.

3. Product idea

A game with levels. Each level is one full practice loop and can be replayed. Points, a daily skill streak and an optional Compete mode bring her back.

4. Choosing a mode

After setup she picks Learn individually or Compete mode. She can switch at any time, and her streak and points stay with her.

5. Levels

Every level is labelled Easy, Medium or Hard. One level is the whole loop:

Learn: a few words with article, English meaning and an example sentence. Not marked.
Practise: fully marked.
Make mistakes: wrong answers are saved.
Get feedback: right answer shown straight away with a short reason.
Revisit: missed words come back inside the same level.
Improve: marked again, on the words she got wrong the first time.
Keep practising: missed words carry into later levels and a review level.

Each level has its own question style. English and German only. Levels can be replayed, with fewer points each time.

Passing: one combined score from Practise and Improve. She passes at 75 percent.

6. Points
Points for every level passed
A clean pass earns more than a pass with mistakes
Points can restore a broken skill streak
7. Skill streak

She earns the day’s streak by doing either of these:

completing one entire level, or
moving from Learn into Practise within 30 minutes

If she does neither, the streak breaks. Points can restore it.

8. Compete mode
Groups of about 10, assigned automatically by streak level
A separate group for new learners with very short streaks
Ranked by streak length inside the group
Up to 3 Connect requests at a time, each expiring after 2 days
If accepted, the two compete head to head on the same level, each in their own time, with scores compared
9. Privacy and safety
Everyone appears under a generated anonymous name and avatar, and identities are never revealed, even after connecting
No real name, photo, phone, email, city or workplace is shown anywhere
Before accepting a request she sees only alias, avatar, streak and levels completed
Block and report on every alias
Only the data needed to run progress and groups is kept
10. What makes it different
Learn always comes first, so nobody is tested on words she hasn’t seen
Medical content built around a nurse’s work
A different question style in each level, with difficulty shown upfront
Mistakes become a visible review level, and the Improve score shows real learning
Two ways to learn: quietly alone, or with an anonymous group at a similar streak
11. Scope for the 3 hour build

Build: setup and mode choice, Learn step, 3 to 4 level types, difficulty labels, scoring for Practise and Improve, points, mistake tracking, review level, skill streak with restore.
Fake with sample data: groups, leaderboard, Connect and head to head, using anonymous dummy learners. Say so openly in your write up.

12. Metrics
Return on day 2 and day 3
Streak length
Accuracy on words she missed before
Levels replayed
Share choosing Compete mode, and whether they return more
13. Risks
Wrong German content
Too many game features and too little learning
Streak feeling like pressure
Rude behaviour between anonymous learners
A loophole in the 30 minute route: if reaching Practise is enough, she can click through Learn without reading anything. A simple fix is to require that she answers at least a few Practise questions. Worth a line in your write up either way.
